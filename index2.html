01 — ABOUT

More than just a college.

NIT Meghalaya is an Institute of National Importance, established in 2010 under the NIT Act.

National Institute of Technology Meghalaya is one of the 31 National Institutes of Technology in India.

The institute focuses on engineering, science, technology, research and innovation while encouraging students to develop academically and personally.

Today, the institute's permanent campus is located at Saitsohpen, Sohra, in the East Khasi Hills of Meghalaya.
